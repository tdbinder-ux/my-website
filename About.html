🌲 A Corner Lot in the Forest Foothills

Nestled in the quiet mountain community of Lake Cushman, this one-acre corner lot blends forest serenity with practical living space. Roads border the property on the east and south, giving easy access while preserving privacy behind a natural wall of evergreens. The land rises gently from the wet western edge toward the home, creating a balance between usable ground and protected habitat.

🏠 The Heart of the Property

At the center stands a 1400‑square‑foot manufactured home, positioned for sunlight and views. The front door faces north, opening onto a curved gravel driveway that enters from the south and loops around to a parking and turnaround area.

South of the home sits the utility cluster — an outhouse, an 8×10 shed, and a 4×6 shed — with septic access just in front. The septic drainfield lies east of the home, enclosed within a fenced dog yard.

🌿 Natural Boundaries and Protected Zones

The west side of the property is a designated wetland and buffer zone, shaded by mature trees and native vegetation. This area remains undisturbed, serving as a natural privacy screen and wildlife corridor.

On the northeast corner, a small easement provides access for utilities and must remain clear of structures — a quiet, shaded space ideal for native plantings and moss paths.

🌞 Usable Land and Garden Space

The east and southeast sides of the property offer the best sunlight and drainage — perfect for gardening. This zone includes:

  • Raised vegetable beds
  • Berry patch and fruit trees
  • Greenhouse site (planned)
  • Herb garden near the home

🛠️ Future Plans

Most of the smaller trees to the south and southeast will be thinned to open space for gardens and fencing, while a few larger trees will remain to preserve shade and habitat. A perimeter fence is planned approximately five feet inside the property line, carefully routed to avoid the wetland buffer and easement.

🌾 A Living Landscape

This property is more than a home site — it’s a living system. Native plants stabilize the wetland edge, pollinator gardens brighten the entry, and raised beds bring fresh food to the table. Every zone has a purpose, from the shaded forest edge to the sunny southeast garden.
