Welcome to Emberroot Haven

Emberroot Haven is the beginning of a long‑held dream — a small, resilient food garden growing on just over an acre in the Lake Cushman forest...

Our Mission

  • Grow organically with soil‑first practices.
  • Build a year‑round food supply through preservation.
  • Develop the garden slowly and sustainably.
  • Honor the wetlands, wildlife, and big trees that define this land.

The Vision

Over time, Emberroot Haven will grow into a layered food system...

The Phased Plan

  • Now: Tomato starts, herbs, planning, gathering supplies.
  • First Season: Initial beds, trellises, temporary deer protection.
  • Year 1: Permanent fencing, expanded beds, first perennials.
  • Year 3: Greenhouse, full perennial patch, established pantry system.
