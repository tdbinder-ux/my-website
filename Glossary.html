Filter Terms

Raised Bed

A framed garden bed elevated above ground level, improving drainage, soil control, and accessibility.

Trellis

A vertical support structure used for climbing plants such as tomatoes, beans, and cucumbers.

Cold Frame

A low, enclosed structure with a clear lid that protects plants from cold temperatures and extends the growing season.

Hoop House

A tunnel-shaped structure covered with plastic that provides warmth, wind protection, and season extension.

Deer Fence

A tall barrier—typically 7–8 feet high—designed to keep deer out of garden areas and protect crops.

Microclimate

A small area within the garden that has unique temperature, moisture, or light conditions compared to surrounding areas.

Companion Planting

The practice of growing certain plants together to improve growth, deter pests, or enhance flavor.

Succession Planting

Planting new crops at intervals throughout the season to ensure continuous harvests.

Frost Date

The average date of the last spring frost or first fall frost, used to plan planting schedules.

Zone 8b

The USDA hardiness zone for the Lake Cushman region, with winter lows between 15–20°F, guiding plant selection.

Mulch

Organic or inorganic material spread over soil to retain moisture, suppress weeds, and regulate temperature.

Compost

Decomposed organic matter used to enrich soil, improve structure, and support healthy plant growth.

Loam

A balanced soil type containing sand, silt, and clay, ideal for most garden plants.

Drainage

The soil’s ability to allow water to move through it, preventing root rot and promoting healthy growth.

Soil pH

A measure of soil acidity or alkalinity, affecting nutrient availability and plant health.

Hardening Off

The gradual process of acclimating indoor-grown seedlings to outdoor conditions before transplanting.

Perennial

A plant that lives for multiple years, returning each growing season from the same root system.

Annual

A plant that completes its entire life cycle—sprouting, flowering, and dying—within a single growing season.
