The Emberroot Garden Project

Emberroot Haven is a forest-edge garden project designed to integrate ecological awareness, intentional layout, and thematic visual elements. It emphasizes soil restoration, native plant layering, and long-term sustainability. The project's name draws on renewal symbolism — ember and phoenix motifs — without affecting its practical function.

Project Goals

  • Restore soil health through layered planting
  • Integrate native species for ecological balance
  • Create a visually cohesive theme inspired by ember-root imagery
